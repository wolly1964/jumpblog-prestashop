# Jump Blog for PrestaShop

**An AI-written blog inside your shop, plus product SEO, with no technical work after setup.**

Jump Blog writes and publishes a new article on your PrestaShop blog every day. The articles are real HTML pages inside your shop (`/blog`), not a JavaScript widget, so Google can index them. ChatGPT and Gemini can read them as well. A good blog doesn't only bring visits: it lifts the SEO of the whole shop.

> **Pricing:** the module is free to download, but Jump Blog is a **subscription service**, with a **15-day free trial**.
> Plans start at R$ 49/month (about €8 / $9 per month, depending on your bank's exchange rate). Details at [jumpblog.com.br](https://jumpblog.com.br).

## Features

**Blog**
- New articles published automatically, written for the keywords your customers really search for
- Real pages inside your shop, with automatic sitemap, hreflang and JSON-LD
- Multilingual: articles in your shop languages
- Deleting an article in Jump Blog also removes it from your shop

**Jump SEO (products)**
- Optimizes meta titles, meta descriptions, product descriptions and image captions, in all languages
- SEO score (Google) and GEO score (ChatGPT / Gemini) for each product
- **You choose which fields it may change.** If you wrote your descriptions by hand, switch "Description" off and they stay untouched
- **Undo** restores the original texts
- Never touches product names, URLs, prices or codes

## Installation (2 minutes)

1. Create a free account at [jumpblog.com.br](https://jumpblog.com.br) and choose **Connect your site → PrestaShop**.
2. Download the module: **[jumpblog-prestashop.zip](https://github.com/wolly1964/jumpblog-prestashop/raw/main/jumpblog-prestashop.zip)** (also on [jumpblog.com.br](https://jumpblog.com.br/assets/jumpblog-prestashop.zip)), then upload it in your back office under **Modules → Module Manager → Upload a module**.
3. Open **Jump Blog → Configure**, paste your connection code (it starts with `JB1`) and click **Connect**.

That's it. Jump Blog studies your shop, finds your keywords and starts writing.

## Module version

Current version: **1.4.1** (PrestaShop 1.7.7 or later). The module only talks to the Jump Blog platform: it has no import from other blog services and no third-party code.
